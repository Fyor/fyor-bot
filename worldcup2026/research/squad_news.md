# 2026 World Cup — Squad & Injury News (top 12 contenders)

Compiled 2026-06-10 (tournament starts 2026-06-11). All items verified against live web sources via search on 2026-06-10; source + date noted per item. Groups per FIFA: 12 groups A–L; final squads (26 players) were due to FIFA by June 1.

## Spain (Group H — Uruguay, Cape Verde, Saudi Arabia) — title favourite ~5.5
- Lamine Yamal: injured left hamstring on 22 Apr (penalty for Barcelona); skipped the Iraq warm-up in A Coruña, but coach De la Fuente (ESPN, ~8 Jun) said he has "no doubt" Yamal will be ready for the opener vs Cape Verde on 15 Jun.
- Rodri (2024 Ballon d'Or) is back to full fitness after missing almost all of 2024-25; expected to be first name on the team sheet (squad named 25 May).
- Nico Williams and Mikel Merino carried fitness doubts into camp; De la Fuente expects both ready. Gavi and Merino were recalled after injury layoffs.
- Historic call: NO Real Madrid players in a Spain World Cup squad for the first time — Dani Carvajal (injury-hit season) and Dean Huijsen dropped (France24/ESPN, 25 May).
- Market positioning: Mikel Oyarzabal is Spain's shortest-priced Golden Boot candidate (19.0 FanDuel, shorter than Yamal), consistent with him leading the line and taking penalties as at Euro 2024.

## France (Group I — Norway, Senegal, Iraq) — ~5.75-6.0
- Kylian Mbappé (captain, main striker, penalty taker) IS in the squad (named 14 May) despite a thigh injury sustained in May with Real Madrid; he cut short one training session in camp (africasoccer, early Jun) but Deschamps told Telefoot before the Northern Ireland warm-up that "all 26 players will be available" (~7-8 Jun).
- Ousmane Dembélé (2025 Ballon d'Or winner) returned to training and was set to feature in the final warm-up; William Saliba cleared of injury fears (FOX, ~7 Jun).
- France lost 2-1 to Ivory Coast in a friendly on 4 Jun — prediction markets moved on it (PredictionNews, 10 Jun); France 16.2% on Polymarket, still co-favourite with Spain.
- Attack depth: Doué, Olise, Cherki, Akliouche all in — arguably the deepest attacking pool in the field.

## England (Group L — Croatia, Ghana, Panama) — ~7.0-8.0
- Harry Kane (captain, striker, penalties) fit and in elite form: 36 goals in 31 Bundesliga games for Bayern in 2025-26 (LiveScore/Sky, squad named 22 May). 2nd favourite for the Golden Boot (8.0).
- Shock omissions by Tuchel: Cole Palmer (form/fitness), Phil Foden, Trent Alexander-Arnold, Maguire and Shaw all left out (Sky Sports/Goal, 22 May); Noni Madueke in.
- Bukayo Saka has an Achilles problem — Tuchel will manage his minutes (ESPN squad piece, late May). Saka/Rice/Eze/Madueke joined camp late (1 Jun) after Arsenal's Champions League final defeat on 30 May.
- Jude Bellingham in despite indifferent Real Madrid form and injury concerns during the season.
- Opener 17 Jun vs Croatia (AT&T Stadium, Texas).

## Brazil (Group C — Morocco, Scotland, Haiti) — ~9.0-10.0
- Injury crisis in attack: Rodrygo OUT (torn ACL + meniscus, early March); Estêvão OUT (grade-4 hamstring, April); Éder Militão OUT (thigh surgery, April) (ESPN injury tracker / Goal, June).
- Neymar (34) recalled by Ancelotti after 2.5 years out of the squad (named 18 May) — but a grade-2 calf strain ruled him out of the warm-ups and he is DOUBTFUL for the 13 Jun opener vs Morocco (FourFourTwo/Olympics.com, early Jun).
- Vinícius Júnior moved from left wing to CENTRAL STRIKER by Ancelotti — the main attacking reference (Olympics.com, June). Golden Boot 26.0.
- Omitted despite fitness/form: João Pedro (20-goal Chelsea season), Richarlison, Savinho, Joelinton.
- Ancelotti's preferred shape is a 4-2-4 with nine forwards in the squad (Raphinha, Martinelli, Cunha, Endrick, Igor Thiago, Luiz Henrique, Rayan).

## Argentina (Group J — Austria, Algeria, Jordan) — holders, ~10.0-11.0
- Messi IS playing — record 6th World Cup at age (almost) 39; captain and headline of the 26-man squad named 28 May (SI/beIN).
- Injury scare resolved: thigh discomfort vs Philadelphia on 24 May (subbed 72'); tests ruled out any tear; Scaloni (early Jun): "Leo is working very well in training and can get minutes in the friendlies" — minutes being managed into the opener 16 Jun vs Algeria.
- 17 of the 2022 winners return (Enzo Fernández, Mac Allister, Julián Álvarez, etc.). Julián Álvarez leads the line (Golden Boot 36.0); Messi on penalties.
- Surprise omissions: Franco Mastantuono left out; veteran Marcos Acuña also cut (beIN, 28 May).

## Portugal (Group K — Colombia, DR Congo, Uzbekistan) — ~9.0-11.0
- Cristiano Ronaldo (40) WILL play, including the opener: FIFA suspended (probation, 1 year) the final two games of his 3-match ban for elbowing Ireland's Dara O'Shea in Nov 2025 qualifying; one game was served vs Armenia (TNT Sports/ESPN). SI notes the reprieve creates "risk of legal action" — minor controversy, not a sporting doubt.
- Martínez backs Ronaldo as starter/captain: "25 goals in his last 30 games" for Portugal; he remains first-choice penalty taker.
- Squad otherwise at full strength around the Ronaldo question; market wobble is about chemistry, not injuries (SI group-bets piece, ~9 Jun).

## Germany (Group E — Ecuador, Ivory Coast, Curaçao) — ~15.0
- Jamal Musiala has recovered from the broken leg suffered at the 2025 Club World Cup; Nagelsmann (squad announcement, 21 May): "Jamal has been getting better and better."
- Late injury: 18-year-old Bayern midfielder Lennart Karl OUT — muscle-bundle tear in left thigh in training (early Jun); replaced by RB Leipzig's Assan Ouédraogo (FourFourTwo).
- Manuel Neuer (40) is the No. 1; Marc-André ter Stegen NOT in the squad.
- Wirtz + Musiala are the creative core; Germany cruised qualifying 5W from 6.

## Netherlands (Group F — Japan, Sweden, Tunisia) — ~21.0-23.0
- Jurriën Timber OUT of the World Cup — persistent groin injury; withdrew after being named, Sunderland's Lutsharel Geertruida called up (FIFA.com/SI, early Jun).
- Xavi Simons OUT — ruptured ACL at Tottenham in late April (ESPN injury tracker).
- Memphis Depay (all-time NL top scorer) is the main striker; attack widely viewed as the squad's weak spot (FourFourTwo, June). Golden Boot 51.0.
- Koeman's 26 is Premier League-heavy; Van Dijk anchors the defence.

## Norway (Group I — France, Senegal, Iraq) — ~36.0; first WC since 1998
- Erling Haaland fit and firing: played the 7 Jun friendly vs Morocco; 55 international goals, 16 in qualifying (top scorer of UEFA qualifying); first World Cup (Olympics.com/OneFootball, June). Golden Boot 13.0-15.0.
- Martin Ødegaard (captain) is the worry: season plagued by fitness issues, missed the March friendlies; fresh from captaining Arsenal to the Premier League title (squad named 21 May).
- Without Ødegaard's guile, previews flag a creativity drop-off as Norway's biggest vulnerability.

## Belgium (Group G — Egypt, Iran, New Zealand) — ~36.0-41.0
- Kevin De Bruyne (34, Napoli) selected for a 4th World Cup but was sidelined with an eye injury when the squad was named 15 May (beIN).
- Romelu Lukaku (33) is a stated gamble by Rudi Garcia: roughly one hour of competitive football all season due to recurring hamstring/muscle injuries at Napoli; still Belgium's all-time top scorer (89 goals) and likely first-choice 9 if fit (World Soccer Talk, 15 May).
- Thibaut Courtois fit and in goal; Youri Tielemans captains the side.
- Market take: Belgium -225 for the group but analysts prefer Egypt (+333) given Belgian reliance on ageing stars (SI, ~9 Jun).

## Colombia (Group K — Portugal, DR Congo, Uzbekistan) — ~41.0
- James Rodríguez (34, captain) had a minor knock that cost him a couple of training sessions in camp; coach Lorenzo (early Jun): "he's doing great now" (ESPN/MLSSoccer, squad 25 May).
- Luis Díaz arrives in the form of his career — moved to Bayern in summer 2025 and won the Bundesliga (Olympics.com, June).
- Striker note: Jhon Durán LEFT OUT of the 26 (beIN, 25 May).

## Uruguay (Group H — Spain, Cape Verde, Saudi Arabia) — ~51.0-66.0
- Darwin Núñez leads the line despite having no club minutes for Al Hilal since February — Bielsa trusts him anyway (FourFourTwo, June).
- Rodrigo Bentancur: serious hamstring injury, out since January; race against time and only "likely to be involved" if recovered (FourFourTwo, June).
- Valverde (Real Madrid) and Ugarte (Man Utd) anchor midfield; odds drifted from 50-1 to 65-1 in the final week (ESPN, ~9 Jun) — market reads Uruguay as weakening.
