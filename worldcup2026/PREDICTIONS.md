## Methodology Notes

**Evidence base for the model design:**
- Bookmaker-consensus models (Leitner, Zeileis & Hornik 2010; Zeileis et al. WC 2014/18/22) consistently outperform Elo-only models at World Cup prediction
- Hvattum & Arnesen (2010): Elo provides the best single-metric baseline for result prediction
- Dixon & Coles (1997): bivariate Poisson with low-score adjustment; rho=-0.10 calibrated on WC data (2.27-2.69 goals/game, 2010-2022)
- Power de-vig (rather than proportional) corrects for favourite-longshot bias in football outrights
- Kicktipp-optimal picks: always predict the score maximising E[pts]; draws are rarely optimal (only when draw probability >35%+ and goal symmetry supports it)
